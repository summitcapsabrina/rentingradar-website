RentingRadar vs Airbitrage.ai

Updated August 10, 2026·6 min read

Quick answer

RentingRadar and Airbitrage.ai solve different parts of the same rental arbitrage workflow. Airbitrage.ai is a marketplace that sources pre-vetted, arbitrage-approved properties for you, priced at $332/month. RentingRadar is a CRM that analyzes, negotiates, and operates deals you find anywhere — starting at $9.99/month with a 7-day free trial.

If you've searched for rental arbitrage software, you've likely run into both names. They get compared often, but they're not really competing for the same job — one helps you find a deal, the other helps you run one. Here's what each actually does, sourced directly from their own sites.

What RentingRadar does

RentingRadar is an AI-powered CRM built specifically for rental arbitrage and co-hosting operators. You bring the property — imported in one click from Zillow, Apartments.com, or HotPads, or added by hand — and RentingRadar takes it from there: up to 30 Airbnb comps per property, a Deal Temperature score (No-Go to Slam-Dunk), rent/concession negotiation forecasting, and full post-lease operations (maintenance, VA scheduling, Airbnb case resolution, guest reviews). Sabrina, a built-in AI expert trained specifically on rental arbitrage and co-hosting, answers questions throughout.

What Airbitrage.ai does

Airbitrage.ai is a marketplace, not a CRM. Per its own site, it connects investors with a browsable catalog of properties already vetted and pre-approved by landlords for Airbnb subleasing, plus a dedicated Sourcing Manager who finds custom properties matching your criteria. It includes profitability calculators, regulatory information, saved searches with daily notifications, and access to mentorship from Steam & Stream, a short-term-rental company with $10M in assets and 700+ students. Its published pricing is a single Pro Investor Plan at $332/month, or $995 upfront for a 3-month term, with payment plans via Klarna or Affirm and a current promotion of one free month with purchase.

Which one do you actually need?

  • You already have deal flow (your own network, Zillow/Apartments.com searches, a broker) and need to analyze, negotiate, and operate it — that's RentingRadar's job.
  • You don't have deal flow yet and want pre-vetted, landlord-approved properties handed to you — that's what Airbitrage.ai is built for.
  • You want both — some operators use Airbitrage.ai to source a property, then run the deal analysis, negotiation, and day-to-day operations in RentingRadar. The two aren't mutually exclusive.

Frequently asked questions

Is Airbitrage.ai cheaper than RentingRadar?

No. Airbitrage.ai's Pro Investor Plan runs $332/month; RentingRadar's three plans run $9.99–$29.99/month. They're priced differently because they include different things — Airbitrage.ai bundles property sourcing and mentorship, RentingRadar doesn't.

Does RentingRadar find me rental arbitrage properties?

No. RentingRadar analyzes, negotiates, and operates deals you bring to it. If you need a service that sources properties for you, that's Airbitrage.ai's role.

Can I use RentingRadar with a property I found through Airbitrage.ai?

Yes. RentingRadar works with any property regardless of where you found it, so the two tools can be used together rather than as an either/or choice.
