# Pricing — RentingRadar

Last updated: 2026-11-06

RentingRadar is the AI-powered CRM for rental arbitrage and co-hosting: deal analysis, negotiation, and operations in one place. All prices in USD. 7-day free trial, card required, then $9.99–$29.99/mo depending on plan unless you cancel. First subscription only. Cancel anytime, no contract.

## Basic
- Price: $9.99/month ($89.99/year, billed annually)
- Limits: 1 deal analysis per month, 1 Operations property, up to 5 Co-Hosting properties
- Features: one-click listing import (Zillow, Apartments.com, HotPads), Deal Temperature scoring, Sabrina AI expert

## Standard
- Price: $19.99/month ($199.99/year, billed annually)
- Limits: 10 deal analyses per month, up to 5 Operations properties, up to 10 Co-Hosting properties
- Features: everything in Basic, plus CSV import/export, dark mode/themes

## Pro
- Price: $29.99/month ($289.99/year, billed annually)
- Limits: unlimited deal analyses, unlimited Operations properties, unlimited Co-Hosting properties
- Features: everything in Standard, plus Negotiation Forecasting Tools (rent dial + free-rent-week concession modeling), full operations management (maintenance, VA scheduling, Airbnb case resolution, guest reviews), priority support

## Notes
- Annual billing saves up to 25% vs. monthly on every tier.
- Every plan includes unlimited Houses and Apartments (arbitrage prospecting) properties. The tiered counts are deal analyses per month, properties in Operations, and properties in Co-Hosting. Hitting a limit never removes anything; you just can't add more until you upgrade.
- No free tier. 7-day free trial, card required, then $9.99–$29.99/mo depending on plan unless you cancel. First subscription only.
- No long-term contracts. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime.
