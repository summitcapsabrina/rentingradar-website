# Pricing — RentingRadar

Last updated: 2026-08-10

RentingRadar is the AI-powered CRM for rental arbitrage and co-hosting: deal analysis, negotiation, and operations in one place. All prices in USD. Every plan includes a 7-day free trial — no charge until the trial ends, cancel anytime, no contract.

## Basic
- Price: $9.99/month ($89.99/year, billed annually)
- Limits: 1 deal analysis per month
- Features: one-click listing import (Zillow, Apartments.com, HotPads), Deal Temperature scoring, Sabrina AI expert

## Standard
- Price: $19.99/month ($199.99/year, billed annually)
- Limits: 10 deal analyses per month
- Features: everything in Basic, plus CSV import/export, dark mode/themes

## Pro
- Price: $29.99/month ($289.99/year, billed annually)
- Limits: unlimited deal analyses
- Features: everything in Standard, plus full operations management (maintenance, VA scheduling, Airbnb case resolution, guest reviews), priority support

## Notes
- Annual billing saves up to 25% vs. monthly on every tier.
- No free tier; the free trial is 7 days on any paid plan, no credit card charge until it ends.
- No long-term contracts. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime.
