Frequently asked questions

How much does RentingRadar cost?

Three monthly plans: Basic at $9.99/month, Standard at $19.99/month, and Pro at $29.99/month. Paying yearly saves up to 25% on any plan.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — every plan includes a 7-day free trial. You're not charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel anytime before that with no charge.

Can I cancel or change plans anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts or long-term commitments. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time, and your data stays safe regardless of your plan.
